The yellow cross is up, but from the side the cube still looks chaotic. Each
cross edge has a second sticker facing outwards, and it belongs above the
center of the same color. This step puts all four home — white stays on the
bottom, yellow on top, as always.

<img src="/diagrams/steps/step_5_yedges.svg" alt="Goal state: yellow cross with every edge's side sticker matching the center below it" width="60" />

## Find the matching pair

Turn **U** one click at a time, a full circle, and at each stop count how many
edge side stickers match the center directly below them. One angle gives you
either:

- **All four match** — this step is done, go to Step 6.
- **Exactly two match** — every other outcome. Three is impossible: if three
  edges are home, the fourth has only one place left to be.

Leave U at the two-match angle and check whether the matching pair sits
**adjacent** (side by side) or **opposite** (facing across the cube).

## The tool: Sune + U

<AlgText moves="R U R' U R U2 R'" /> followed by a single `U`.

Held normally, this swaps the **front and left** edges while leaving the back
and right edges — and the entire yellow cross — untouched. It scrambles the
corners; ignore them completely, they are Steps 6 and 7.

<TwistyPlayer alg="R U R' U R U2 R' U" anchor="start" />

*Sune + U — watch the front and left edges trade places.*

## Adjacent pair

<img src="/diagrams/steps/align_adjacent.svg" alt="Two matching edges side by side — hold them at the back and right" width="60" />

Hold the cube so the two matching edges sit at the **back and right**. Run
Sune, then `U`. The front and left edges trade places, and all four edges are
home.

## Opposite pair

<img src="/diagrams/steps/align_diagonal.svg" alt="Two matching edges facing each other across the cube" width="60" />

No grip puts an opposite pair at back-and-right, so run Sune + `U` from
**any** angle. It won't solve the edges — it converts the case: turn U again
to find the new matching pair, which is now adjacent, hold it back-and-right,
and apply once more.

<Callout type="caution">
  The yellow cross must survive this step untouched. If yellow stickers have
  left the top face, a turn slipped mid-algorithm — rebuild the cross
  (Step 4) and retry.
</Callout>

Repeat the loop — turn U to count matches, hold the matching pair
back-and-right, Sune + `U` — until all four side stickers match their
centers. Adjacent takes one round, opposite takes two. Step 6 then positions
the corners.
