You started with one trigger and a seven-step beginner method. You now run
complete 2-look CFOP: 19 algorithms in daily use — 22 learned, three of them
beginner tools the switch retired — and every last-layer case solved in
**one look, one algorithm**. No more chaining Sune and hoping — you recognize,
you execute, you move on.

## Honest expectations

- **Sub-60 seconds** within a few weeks of regular practice. Nothing new to
  learn — recognition speed and pause-free turning are the bottleneck now.
- **Sub-30** needs F2L. The first two layers eat most of your solve time, and
  no last-layer algorithm buys that back.

## What's next, in order

Full CFOP is three more lessons, and they are the next three pages of this
course. Take them in this order — the ordering is the advice:

1. [F2L: Solve Two Layers at Once](/learn/f2l-intuition/) — the single
   biggest unlock, and the one that gets you under 30 seconds.
2. [Full PLL: 21 Cases as Upgrades](/learn/full-pll/) — fifteen upgrades that
   each collapse a two-algorithm finish into one.
3. [Full OLL: 57 Cases by Shape](/learn/full-oll-overview/) — worth having,
   never urgent. Learn it last.

## How to practice now

1. **Slow solves.** Turn at half speed and never stop moving. Speed is
   eliminated pauses, not fast fingers.
2. **Look ahead.** During any algorithm you can do without watching — Sune
   first — keep your eyes hunting for the next case before this one finishes.
3. **Drill hesitations.** Any case that makes you think, set it up and solve
   it ten times straight. One slow case costs more than ten fast ones save.

<Callout type="tip">
  Time full solves once or twice a week, not every session. Daily practice
  should be untimed and deliberate — the timer measures progress, it doesn't
  create it.
</Callout>
