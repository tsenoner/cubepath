Hold the cube **white center up** — for this step only. The goal: four white
edges around the white center, each edge's second color sitting directly
above its matching center. A white plus with wrong side colors is not a
cross — check the sides, not just the top.

## The direct method

One edge at a time:

1. **Find a white edge**, anywhere on the cube.
2. **Read its other color.** That color names its home — the white-red
   edge belongs above the red center, nowhere else.
3. **Bring it under that center**, in the bottom layer with white facing
   down. Turn the bottom layer until the edge sits directly below its
   matching center.
4. **Turn that face twice.** The edge travels straight up into the cross,
   white on top, color matching the center.

If an edge arrives at the top with white facing sideways instead of up,
it's flipped: one turn drops it back out, and you re-approach it through
the bottom layer as above.

## Why there's no algorithm

Nothing else is solved yet, so every turn is free — you cannot break what
does not exist. The only constraint grows as you work: don't knock out edges
you've already placed, and if a move would, turn the blocker out of the way
first, insert, then restore it.

Repeat until all four edges are in. Done means: white cross on top, and
each side face shows its edge sticker matching the center below it.

## Flip it

<Figure
  size="side"
  src="/diagrams/steps/step_flip.svg"
  alt="Cube flipped upside down so white faces the table and yellow faces up"
  caption="x2 — and it stays this way to the end."
/>

Turn the whole cube upside down: `x2`. White is now on the bottom, yellow
on top — **and stays that way for the rest of the solve**. Every later
step assumes this grip: you work in the top layer, where you can see
pieces coming, and drop them down into place.

<Callout type="tip">
  Once the cross feels easy, stop pausing between edges: while inserting
  one, find the next with your eyes. Eventually you'll place all four in
  one flow — the seed of cross planning, where speedcubers map the entire
  cross before their first turn.
</Callout>
