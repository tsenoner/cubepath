Two layers down. From here you stop chasing individual pieces and start
reading **patterns** on the top face. The goal: all four yellow edges
pointing up — a yellow plus.

Hold the cube white on bottom, yellow on top. Look only at the yellow
center and the four edges around it. Corners don't matter yet — grey in
the diagrams means "don't care", so ignore any yellow corners you happen
to see.

## One algorithm, three states

The whole step is **F-sexy-F'**: an F, the sexy move you already own, F'
to undo:

<AlgText moves="F R U R' U' F'" />

The F hides a top edge inside the front layer, the sexy move kicks it
back out flipped and tucks a second one in, and F' restores everything
else — net effect, exactly two edges reoriented per pass. Ignoring
corners, only three starting patterns exist:

<Figure src="/diagrams/oll/oll_dot.svg" alt="Dot case — only the yellow center faces up" caption="Dot — three passes" />

<Figure src="/diagrams/oll/oll_hook.svg" alt="Hook case — yellow center plus two adjacent edges forming an L" caption="Hook — two passes" />

<Figure src="/diagrams/oll/oll_line.svg" alt="Line case — yellow center plus two opposite edges in a row" caption="Line — one pass" />

The count is how many passes you need: each pass advances one state,
**Dot → Hook → Line → Cross**.

## The angle matters

<Callout type="caution">
  Set the angle *before* each pass — spin the top face with U turns until
  the pattern sits right:

  - **Dot** — any angle.
  - **Hook** — the L in the **back-left**: the two yellow edges point to
    the back and to the left.
  - **Line** — **horizontal**, running left to right.

  Re-read the top face after every pass. The next state rarely lands at
  the correct angle on its own.
</Callout>

Repeat — read the state, set the angle, run the algorithm — until the
yellow plus appears. The side colors of those edges won't match the side
centers yet; that's correct, and it's exactly what Step 5 fixes. Already
have the cross? Skip ahead.
