In Phase 2 you chained Sune until the corners fell into place. Now you finish
the job properly: **one look, one algorithm**. Anti-Sune completes the Sune
pair, and the remaining five cases each get a dedicated algorithm.

Recognition comes first. After the yellow cross, look at the four corners and
count how many already show yellow on top — that number narrows it to one or
two cases, and the side stickers settle it.

## How to tell the look-alikes apart

- **0 yellow corners** — check the headlights: on the left *and* right it's
  **Double Headlights**; on the left only, it's **Pi**.
- **2 yellow corners** — adjacent (right column) is **Chameleon**; diagonal is
  **Bowtie**; if the two unsolved corners show headlights facing you, it's
  **Headlights**.
- **1 yellow corner** — Sune or Anti-Sune, by where the solved corner sits.

<Callout type="tip">
  Anti-Sune is Sune backwards. If you can already do Sune smoothly, practice
  reading `R U2 R' U' R U' R'` as one motion rather than seven letters.
</Callout>
