Yellow cross done, edges matched to their centers — the cube is four corners
from solved. This step drags each corner into its correct **slot**; making
yellow face up is Step 7's job. Keep white on the bottom, yellow on top.

## What "positioned" means

A corner is **positioned** when its three sticker colors match the three
faces it touches. The corner sitting between the red and green faces must be
the yellow-red-green piece — in *any* twist. Red or green on top still
counts: right place, wrong rotation, and rotation waits until Step 7.

Check all four top corners this way. The edges are already aligned, so every
side face shows its true color and you can read each slot straight off the
centers.

<Callout type="caution">
  While you check, turn the **whole cube** (`y`), never the top layer alone —
  a bare `U` turn wrecks the edges you aligned in Step 5.
</Callout>

## Niklas

One algorithm handles everything:

<AlgText moves="R U' L' U R' U' L" />

**Niklas** holds the front-left corner in its seat and cycles the other
three: front-right → back-right → back-left → front-right. The bottom two
layers come back untouched.

## The routine

1. **Find a positioned corner** and turn the whole cube until it sits at the
   **front-left** of the top layer.
2. **Apply Niklas**, then square the top with `U` so the edges match again.
3. **Look again.** All four positioned → Step 7. Otherwise the front-left
   corner is still home — apply Niklas once more. A second pass sends the
   three corners around the other way, so two passes always finish it.

**No positioned corner anywhere?** Apply Niklas from any angle and re-square
with `U`. Exactly one corner is now positioned — hold it front-left and
carry on.

<Callout type="info">
  Why this always works: with the edges solved, the four corners can only be
  all placed, exactly one placed (a three-corner cycle), or none placed (two
  pairs swapped). Niklas is itself a three-corner cycle, so it turns "none"
  into "one" — you never need more than three applications in total.
</Callout>

You are done when every corner's three colors match its three faces. The top
still looks scrambled — most corners won't show yellow upward yet. That's
expected: Step 7 twists them in place without moving them.
