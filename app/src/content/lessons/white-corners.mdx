Cross done, cube flipped (`x2`): white on the bottom, yellow on top, and it
stays that way for the rest of the solve. Now place the four white corners.
Each belongs in the bottom layer between the two centers that match its side
colors — all four in, and the first layer is complete.

<img src="/diagrams/steps/step_2_corners.svg" alt="Goal state: white face down with the first layer's side colors matching their centers" width="60" />

## The righty trigger

<AlgText moves="R U R' U'" />

Four moves you will use in every phase of this course — learn it as one
motion, not four letters. `R` lifts the front-right corner slot into the top
layer, `U` feeds a corner in (or swings one out of the way), `R'` `U'` puts
everything back. Watch it run:

<TwistyPlayer alg="R U R' U'" anchor="start" />

## Fetch a corner

Find any corner in the top layer with a white sticker. Its other two colors
name its home — white-red-green lives between the red and green centers. Turn
**only U** until the corner sits directly above that slot, then turn the whole
cube so corner and slot are at the front-right.

## Three cases

Look at where the white sticker points:

| | White faces | Do |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| <img src="/diagrams/steps/corner_right.svg" alt="Corner at front-right with the white sticker facing right" width="60" /> | **Right** | righty ×1 |
| <img src="/diagrams/steps/corner_front.svg" alt="Corner at front-right with the white sticker facing front" width="60" /> | **Front** | `y` — corner and slot are now front-left — then lefty ×1 |
| <img src="/diagrams/steps/corner_up.svg" alt="Corner at front-right with the white sticker facing up" width="60" /> | **Up** | righty ×3 |

Lefty is righty in the mirror, run with the left hand:

<AlgText moves="L' U' L U" />

## The loop

Can't recall the cases? Repeat righty until the corner drops in with white
facing down — it always does. Each pass swaps the corner between the slot and
the top layer, twisting it as it goes: white-right lands in one pass, white-up
in three, white-front in five (which is why the `y` + lefty shortcut exists).
Then turn **only U** to bring the next white corner above its slot, and go
again until all four are in.

<Callout type="caution">
  Mid-trigger the cross looks broken. It isn't — every full pass of righty
  restores the cross and every corner you've already placed. Always finish
  all four moves; between corners, turn only `U`.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
  A corner stuck in the bottom layer — wrong slot, or white not facing down?
  Hold that slot at the front-right and do righty once: the corner pops into
  the top layer. Fetch it as normal.
</Callout>

Done when the bottom face is solid white and each side face's bottom row
matches its center.
