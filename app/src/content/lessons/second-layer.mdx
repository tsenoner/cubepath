First layer done. Hold the cube **white on the bottom, yellow on top** — the
solved layer stays at the bottom from here on. This step fills the four
middle-layer edges. No new algorithms: every insert is built from righty and
lefty.

## Find an edge

Scan the four top-layer edges for one with **no yellow on either sticker** —
that edge belongs in the middle layer. Any edge showing yellow lives on top;
skip it.

Turn **U** until that edge's **front sticker** sits directly above the center
of the same color, and turn the whole cube so that face is toward you.
Together with the solved bottom row, the front face now shows an
**upside-down T** in one color. That T is your starting position for every
insert.

## Read the direction, then insert

Now look at the edge's **top sticker**. It matches either the right or the
left center — that is the slot it belongs in, and it picks the insert:

### Points right

<Figure src="/diagrams/steps/edge_right.svg" alt="Edge inserting into the front-right slot" caption="Top sticker matches the right centre." />

`U`, then <AlgText moves="R U R' U'" /> (righty), then `y`, then
<AlgText moves="L' U' L U" /> (lefty).

### Points left

<Figure src="/diagrams/steps/edge_left.svg" alt="Edge inserting into the front-left slot" caption="Top sticker matches the left centre." />

`U'`, then <AlgText moves="L' U' L U" /> (lefty), then `y'`, then
<AlgText moves="R U R' U'" /> (righty).

`y` rotates the whole cube like a `U` turn — the right face swings round to
face you; `y'` is the reverse.

Why two triggers? No move drops an edge straight into a middle-layer slot, so
the insert borrows the solved corner beside it: the first trigger takes the
corner out and the mirrored one puts it back.

<Callout type="caution">
  Match the **front** sticker first, every time. If you line up the top
  sticker instead, the edge goes in flipped — and you'll be prying it back
  out.
</Callout>

## When the edge is stuck in a slot

Sometimes an edge you need is already in the middle layer — wrong slot, or
right slot but flipped — and nothing usable is left on top. Evict it: hold
the cube with that slot at the **front-right**, put any top-layer edge above
the front center, and run the points-right insert. Inserting the new edge
pops the stuck one out to the top layer, where you can solve it properly.

## Repeat until

Loop the step — find a yellow-free edge, form the T, read the top sticker,
insert — until all four middle edges are home. You are done when every edge
left in the top layer shows yellow: the first two layers are solid, and only
the yellow face remains.
