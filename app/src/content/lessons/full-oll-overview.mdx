Full OLL orients the whole last layer in one look and one algorithm. It is
also the **last** thing to learn in CFOP: finish F2L and full PLL first —
they pay far more per hour of practice. The 57 dedicated algorithms buy you
one look and a handful of moves over 2-look. Worth having; never urgent.

<Callout type="info">
  Nothing breaks while you learn. Any case you haven't studied yet still
  falls to 2-look — orient the edges, then the OCLL you know. Full OLL is a
  replacement, not a prerequisite.
</Callout>

## Recognition by shape

You never memorize 57 pictures. The yellow stickers already oriented on top
form a shape — a dot, a bar, a letter, a fish. The shape narrows 57 cases
to a family of two to eight, and the side stickers settle the exact case.
Each family below shows a few representative cases.

<Callout type="tip">
  Learn by family: pick one, learn its two to eight algorithms, drill only
  that family in the trainer until recognition is instant — then move on.
  Random sampling across all 57 teaches nothing. Start with the short
  families — T, P, W, C — and leave dots for last. Months to finish is
  normal; 2-look covers you the whole way.
</Callout>
