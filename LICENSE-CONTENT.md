## Third-party fonts — SIL Open Font License 1.1

The app ships four webfont files in `app/public/fonts/`. They are unmodified
Google Fonts "latin" subsets, self-hosted so a cold offline load still renders
in the right typefaces. Both families are licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL) — confirmed
from name ID 14 in each file, which reads `http://scripts.sil.org/OFL`. The
full license text and the copyright notices ship next to the fonts, in
`app/public/fonts/OFL.txt`.
